# YouTube Publishing Package

## Required package
- 3 accurate title options; no misleading hooks or keyword stuffing.
- Description with concise summary, chapters, credits, and source references where appropriate.
- Thumbnail brief with one clear focal subject and short readable text; it must represent the actual video.
- Chapter timestamps derived from final video timecodes.
- Optional relevant hashtags/tags; do not over-optimize or promise reach.
- Playlist/category/language recommendations based on the actual content.
- Upload checklist for title, description, subtitles, thumbnail, audience setting, rights, and disclosure.

## Synthetic/altered content
Check current platform disclosure guidance when the video contains realistic altered or synthetic depictions that could mislead viewers. Make disclosure notes explicit; do not assume disclosure is unnecessary.

## Publish gate
Prepare the package only. Do not upload or publish without the user's explicit approval and authorized account access. Never promise virality, monetization, views, or rankings.
