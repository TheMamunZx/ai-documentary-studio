# Audio Design & Mix

## Layers
Narration is the primary intelligibility layer. Add music, ambience, and SFX only when they support the scene's meaning. Do not add dramatic sounds that imply an event or physical property not established by the evidence.

## Cue sheet
For each cue record cue ID, in/out time, scene, type, asset ID, purpose, gain intent, fade/crossfade, ducking, source, license, creator, attribution, and notes.

## Mix QA
- Duck music below narration; verify speech remains clear on headphones and ordinary speakers.
- Avoid abrupt edits, distortion, clipped peaks, excessive bass, and loudness jumps between chapters.
- Check mono compatibility and channel mapping.
- Choose loudness/true-peak targets based on delivery platform and current workflow requirements; do not claim platform compliance without measurement.
- Preserve audio stems where practical.

## Deliverables
`audio_cue_sheet.csv`, `audio_asset_licenses.csv`, `mix_notes.md`, and stems/mix exports.
